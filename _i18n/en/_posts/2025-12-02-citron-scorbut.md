## What worked well

#### Narrative and level design
I really liked adding a narrative aspect to the game. It really changed the feel of the experience.
The core mechanic felt a bit meaningless when testing it alone, but it really came together with
**both level design and a narrative setting**.

#### Brainstorming around a clear fantasy
I am proud of the way brainstorming went for this concept. I achieved a game with a clear appeal that also respected the given constraints I had for the game.

#### Technical Proficiency
It felt really good to be able to build fast on certain features.
- All technical game design stuff was a breeze
- I struggled

## What could be improved

#### Really late working gameplay loop
I spent a lot of times building gameplay elements without knowing if the core gameplay loop would work.
Possible improvements I am thinking of:
 - Iterating on different gameplay loops, selecting the easiest gameplay loop first and then changing it later on to have time to "find the fun".
 - The genre seems to be a really defining choice for this.

#### Milestones & Goals
I didn't plan out clear milestones and goals that would have allowed me to course correct the project and scale over time.
- This led me to a game that feels unbalanced and unfinished.
- But also maybe I would have gone for a safer loop and wouldn't have explored a linear narrative experience. So I wouldn't have learned as much.

#### Missing Essentials
The game lacks sound design, music, a proper end screen and ability to replay.
The game isn't polished at all and visuals aren't what they should be.

### What I want to do on next game jam
- I want to spend more time on polish
- I want to spend more time on visual design
- I want to spend more time on fantasy
- I want to have all of the essentials within the game
- I want to take screenshots or videos at the end of each development doay.
- I didn't have really efficient ways to assess the appeal & engagement of the game
