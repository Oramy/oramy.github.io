The moment to moment gameplay, the narrative and the visuals all participated with that feeling of being an alpinist. I wanted one Cairn screenshots that really conveys the experience of the game.

{% include image-comparison.html image1="/assets/images/ui-training/cairn-study-1.png" image2="/assets/images/ui-training/cairn-study-ref-1.png" %} {%- capture md -%} Overall I think this works pretty well. - Not representing the character leaves the image without its meaning so it is a big flaw of my study. - The image lacks depth, details & texture. - It would have helped to draw clouds with a softer brush & take the time to represent how the light hits them. ### Foreground cliff I am pretty happy with the color blocking of the foreground cliff. We do understand that it is made of rock. It is missing some cracks & roughness to really convey this aspect. I built this foreground by creating the overall shape with the lit color. Then I added the shadows color and finished with the greener rock color & some cracks. I think I should have done: - Blockout silhouette - Place some cracks - Create non-destructive shadows with a product layer - Paint rock textures The colors & contrasts fail to represent the sun hitting & warming up the stones. ### Other elements I need to find the **smudge tool** in Affinity to control my gradients more effectively. To trace out hard edges, **using the lasso tool & polygonal lasso** would have helped defining shapes. This would have helped tracing the line between ground & cliffs in the background for example.
